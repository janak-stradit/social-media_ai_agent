Not sure what to post? **Ideas for you**, on the Studio Chat start screen, suggests posts written for your brand. Click one and its brief is filled in, ready to generate.

## The four tabs

- **From your brand**: ideas drawn from your own products and website.
- **Trending**: this week's most talked-about stories in your industry and market. AVIR AI reads industry news, finds what several publishers are covering, and links each idea to its source.
- **Post types that work**: formats that do well for your kind of business, such as an explainer, a customer story or a quick tip.
- **Upcoming dates**: festivals and national days coming up in your markets (the US, the UAE and India), with a post you could have ready in time.

Ideas follow your brand profile, your industry's advertising rules, and your account type: a personal brand gets first-person ideas, a company gets ideas that fit a team.

## Using and refreshing

- **Click an idea** to load its brief, then edit it or press **Generate**.
- The **refresh** button (the two arrows, next to the tabs) writes a new set if none of them fit.
- Ideas are updated once a day.

## Ideas by email

If your admin has switched them on, you may also get:

- a **weekly email** with three ideas,
- a **reminder** a few days before a festival, or when your industry has a big story,
- a **monthly recap** of what you created.

Every button in these emails opens Studio Chat with that idea loaded. The same reminders appear under the **bell** at the top of the page. Turn the emails off any time in [Settings](/settings#notifications) or with the unsubscribe link in the email.
