Already have a blog post, product page, brochure or report? AVIR AI reads it and suggests 3-5 different post ideas, each using the facts from your content.

## Start

Use any of these:

- On the empty Studio Chat screen, paste a link into **Turn your content into posts** and press **Get ideas**.
- Press the import button next to the paperclip in the composer.
- Paste a link on its own into the brief and press **Turn this link into post ideas**.
- Drag a PDF onto the composer.

## Choose your source

| Tab | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Link | A blog post, product page, case study or news article |
| PDF | A brochure, report or deck saved as PDF, up to 15 MB |
| Paste text | Anything else: a press release, notes, or a page that can't be read from a link |

> Tip: if a website blocks reading, or a PDF is a scan, AVIR AI says so and offers **Paste the text instead**.

Reading takes about 10-30 seconds. Press **Cancel** to stop and choose something else.

## Use the ideas

Each idea shows what kind of post it suits best: a text post, a post with an image, or a carousel.

- **Write this post** loads the idea into the brief. Choose the platforms and press Generate. A carousel idea opens as the `/carousel` command, so the slides are made for you.
- **Edit** lets you change the idea before writing it.
- **Save** keeps the idea under the star in the composer for later.

Your ideas stay in the window after you write one, so you can come back for the others: press the import button again. **Use other content** starts again with a new source.

> Note: the ideas use only the facts in your content. Check names, numbers and prices before you publish.
