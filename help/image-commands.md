Image commands make a specific kind of picture. Type `/` in Studio Chat to see the list, choose one, and send.

## The commands

| Command | What you get | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `/3dbillboard` | Your product popping out of a giant 3D billboard in a city | 16:9 |
| `/metaad` | A Facebook and Instagram ad image, plus the ad copy | 1:1, or all of 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16 |
| `/premiumshowcase` | A luxury studio photoshoot of your product | 1:1 |
| `/lifestyle` | Your product in real, everyday use | 4:5 |
| `/catalog` | A clean white-background shot for online shops | 1:1 |
| `/festive` | Your product dressed up for a festival | 1:1 |
| `/carousel` | 3 to 10 slides that tell one story, with the caption | 1:1 or 4:5 |

## How to use one

1. **Attach a product photo** with the paperclip. Every command except `/carousel` needs one.
2. Type `/` and pick the command.
3. Add any direction you like, for example `/festive Diwali` or `/lifestyle on a kitchen counter in morning light`.
4. Press **Generate**.

Your real logo is placed on the finished image. The AI never redraws it, so it always looks exactly right.

## Carousels

`/carousel` plans the slides first (a hook, your points, a call to action), then draws them all in one matching style and writes the caption and hashtags.

- Say how many slides: `/carousel 7 slides on why live tracking matters`. Without a number you get 5.
- Add `4:5` for portrait slides, which show larger in the feed.
- A product photo is optional.

You can also build a carousel from images you already made: in **Send for approval**, choose **Carousel** and pick the slides in order.

## Changing an image

Type the change as a normal message: *"make the background darker"*, *"remove the plant"*. Each edit becomes a new version, and the earlier ones are kept.

> Note: every image, including each carousel slide, counts toward your daily image limit. The number left today is shown next to **Create**. See [Credits and limits](/help/credits-and-limits).
