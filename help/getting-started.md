AVIR AI turns a one-line brief into ready-to-post captions, hashtags and images for LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook, written in your brand's voice.

## The five steps

1. **Check your brand profile.** When you signed up, AVIR AI read your website and built a profile: what you sell, how you sound, your colours and your logo. Open [Brand profile](/brand-profile) and correct anything that's off. Every post follows it.
2. **Write your first post.** In [Studio Chat](/dashboard), type what you want to say, for example *"Announce our new delivery tracking feature"*. Choose the platforms under **Post to**, then press **Generate**.
3. **Add an image.** Tick **Image** under **Create**, or type `/` to pick an image command such as `/carousel`.
4. **Get it approved.** Click **Send for approval** on the result. Your reviewer gets one email with every platform's version.
5. **Publish.** Connect your accounts in [Social accounts](/settings), then publish the approved post.

> Tip: the **Get started** card in Studio Chat tracks these steps for you and ticks each one off as you do it.

## Good to know

- **Conversations keep their context.** A follow-up like *"make it shorter"* edits the post above it. Click **New Conversation** to start something unrelated.
- **Nothing is posted without you.** AVIR AI creates content; publishing always needs your click.
- **Take the tour again** any time from the menu under your name.
