Your brand profile is why posts sound like you. AVIR AI builds it by reading your website, and uses it for every caption, hashtag and image.

## What's in it

- **Company and industry**: who you are and what you sell.
- **Voice**: how you write, for example warm and practical, or formal and expert.
- **Audience**: who you're talking to.
- **Colours and visual style**: used as accents in generated images.
- **Logo**: your real logo file, placed on images as it is.
- **Products and services**: what posts can talk about.
- **Do's and don'ts**: things to always or never say.

## Checking and editing

Open [Brand profile](/brand-profile), read each section, and fix anything that's wrong. Changes apply to the next post you generate.

Click **Re-analyze** if your website has changed a lot, for example after a rebrand. This reads the site again and fetches your logo afresh.

## Markets and compliance

Your profile also records your **industry** and **markets** (the US, the UAE and India). They decide:

- which advertising rules your captions are checked against. Regulated industries such as finance or healthcare get notes when a caption makes a risky claim;
- which festivals and national days appear in [Ideas for you](/help/ideas-for-you).

Confirm them in the compliance section of your profile.

> Note: on an Enterprise plan the brand is set company-wide by your admin, in Brand Configuration, and everyone shares it.
