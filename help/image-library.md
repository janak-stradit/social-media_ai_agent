The [Image library](/library) keeps every image you create in Studio Chat, newest first. Open it from the menu under your name.

## Finding an image

Type in the search box to find images by what they show, for example *"sneakers"* or *"Diwali"*. The search looks at what you asked for when the image was made.

Click an image to see it larger. From there you can:

- **Use in a new post**: opens Studio Chat with the image attached, ready for you to describe the post.
- **Download** the image.
- **Open its conversation** to go back to where it was made.

## Making a carousel from existing images

1. Tick the images you want, in the order they should appear.
2. Click **Schedule** or **Send for approval** in the bar that appears.
3. Write the caption for each platform and finish as usual.

One ticked image makes a single-image post. Two or more make a carousel: up to 10 slides on Instagram and Facebook, up to 20 on LinkedIn.

> Tip: Instagram needs every slide of a carousel in the same shape. The dialog tells you if the ones you picked don't match.

## Picking library images elsewhere

When you send or schedule a post from Studio Chat and click **Pick image** or **Add slides**, choose **All my images** to pick from the whole library instead of only the current conversation.
