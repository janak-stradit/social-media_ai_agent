The [Image library](/library) keeps every image you create in Studio Chat. Open it from the menu under your name.

## Campaigns, all images and favourites

Use the switch at the top to choose what you see:

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Campaigns | A folder for each Studio Chat conversation that made images, newest first. Images made outside a conversation are in **Other images**. |
| All images | Every image, newest first. |
| Favourites | The images you starred. |

Open a campaign to see its images. From there, **Open conversation** takes you back to where they were made, and **Make a carousel** puts the campaign's images into the carousel tray.

Type in the search box to find campaigns by name, or images by what they show, for example *"sneakers"* or *"Diwali"*.

## Versions

An image you edited appears once, showing its newest version, with a badge such as **3 versions**. Open it to see every version, from the original to the latest, and pick the one you want to use or download.

## Favourites

Click the star on an image to keep it in **Favourites**. Click it again to remove it.

## Looking at one image

Click an image to see it larger, with its date, size and platform. From there you can:

- **Use in a new post**: opens Studio Chat with the image attached, ready for you to describe the post.
- **Add to carousel tray**.
- **Download** the image.
- **Add to favourites**.
- **Open its conversation** to go back to where it was made.
- **Delete** it, with all its versions.

## Making a carousel

1. Tick the images you want, or use **Make a carousel** inside a campaign. They appear in the tray at the bottom of the page.
2. Drag the images in the tray to change their order, or use the arrows. The cross removes one.
3. Click **Schedule** or **Send for approval**, write the caption for each platform and finish as usual.

One image makes a single-image post. Two or more make a carousel: up to 10 slides on Instagram and Facebook, up to 20 on LinkedIn.

> Tip: Instagram needs every slide of a carousel in the same shape. The dialog tells you if the ones you picked don't match.

## Deleting images

Delete one image from its viewer, or several at once with **Delete** in the tray. You have a few seconds to press **Undo**. Deleting removes an image from the library only: posts that already use it keep working.

## Picking library images elsewhere

When you send or schedule a post from Studio Chat and click **Pick image** or **Add slides**, choose **All my images** to pick from the whole library instead of only the current conversation.
