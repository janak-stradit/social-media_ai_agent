Two things are counted on your account: **credits** and a **daily image limit**.

## Credits

Every generation uses some of your credit balance: writing text costs a little, images cost more. Open the menu under your name to see:

- your **credit balance** and limit,
- **tokens used** and total cost so far.

Each result in Studio Chat also shows what it cost.

When the balance runs out, generation pauses. Click **Extend** in the menu to ask your admin for more. You'll get an email when they decide.

## Daily image limit

There's a limit on how many images you can create each day. It counts every new and edited image, including each slide of a carousel.

- The number **left today** is shown next to **Create** in Studio Chat.
- A command that needs several images, such as a 7-slide carousel, checks that you have enough before it starts. Nothing is made, and nothing is charged, if you don't.
- The count resets at midnight UTC.

## Saving credits

- **Rewrite caption** keeps the image you already have and only writes the text again.
- One image is **shared across platforms** by default. Only ask for separate images when you need them.
- Use **Analyze** to check a brief before generating, so the first result is closer to what you want.

> Tip: admins set each person's credit limit, daily image limit and image model in the Admin Control Center.
