A scheduled post is published by AVIR AI at the time you choose. Each platform gets its own caption and media, exactly as you scheduled them.

> Note: if the **Schedule** button shows **Soon**, scheduling from Studio Chat has not been switched on for your workspace yet.

## Scheduling a post

1. In Studio Chat, click **Schedule** under a post.
2. Choose the date and time. Times are in your own time zone.
3. Tick the platforms. Each one shows the account it posts as. A platform that isn't connected has a link to [Social accounts](/settings).
4. For each platform, check the caption and choose **Text only**, **One image** or **Carousel**.
5. Click **Schedule**.

The button then shows when the post goes out, and the post is on your [Content Calendar](/calendar).

> Tip: you don't need to send a post for approval before scheduling it. If someone has to sign it off first, use **Send for approval**, then publish from the approved request.

## On the calendar

Scheduled posts are shown on their day with their time.

- **Move it**: drag the post to another day. It keeps its time of day. Or click it and pick a new date and time under **Move to**.
- **Cancel it**: click the post, then **Cancel this post**.
- **See what happened**: click a published post for a link to it on each platform.

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blue | Scheduled, waiting for its time |
| Green | Published |
| Amber | Published on some platforms only |
| Red | Not published |

## If a post doesn't go out

You get a notification under the bell, with the reason for each platform. Fix the cause (most often an expired connection), then click the post on the calendar and choose **Try again now**. Platforms that were already published are not sent again.

All scheduled posts are also listed in [Social accounts](/settings), under **Scheduled posts**.

## Good to know

- A post can be scheduled up to a year ahead.
- Scheduled posts are checked about three times a minute, so a post goes out within a minute of its time.
- The caption and hashtags must fit the platform's length limit, because nothing is cut short when publishing. See [Studio Chat](/help/studio-chat).
