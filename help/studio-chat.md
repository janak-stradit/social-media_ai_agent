Studio Chat is where posts are made. You describe the post; AVIR AI researches the topic, writes a caption for each platform, picks hashtags and, if you ask, creates the image.

## Writing a brief

One or two sentences are enough. Say **what** the post is about and anything that must be in it.

- *"Announce our Diwali offer: 20% off all orders until Sunday"*
- *"A LinkedIn post on three mistakes companies make with fleet tracking"*
- *"We're hiring a product designer in Dubai"*

Press `Enter` to send, or `Shift` + `Enter` for a new line.

What you type is kept as a draft in your browser until you send it, so a refresh doesn't lose it.

### Briefs you use often

Under a message you sent, click **Save brief** to keep it. Saved briefs are listed behind the star next to the paperclip: click one to load it into the message box. **Use again** under a message starts a new post from that brief straight away, for example for another platform.

## Choosing what to make

- **Post to**: pick one or more of Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube. Each gets its own caption in that platform's style.
- **Create**: **Caption** is on by default. Tick **Image** to get a picture with it.
- **Voice** and **Tone**: leave them on their defaults to follow your brand profile, or change them for a single post.
- **Attach** (the paperclip): add a photo, for example your product. AVIR AI looks at it and uses it in the post or image. You can also drag a photo onto the message box, or paste a copied image or screenshot with `Ctrl` + `V`.
- **Analyze** checks your brief first and shows the research before anything is written.

## Reading the result

Each platform has its own tab with:

- the **caption**, with **Edit** and **Copy** buttons;
- **hashtags** chosen for that platform. Spam tags such as #follow4follow are left out, and once your posts have results, tags that did well on them are reused where they fit;
- **compliance notes**, if your industry has advertising rules that the caption should respect;
- the **image**, if you asked for one. One image is shared across platforms unless you ask for a separate one. **Enlarge** shows it full size.

**Preview post** shows the post the way it will look on each platform, including where a long caption is cut off behind "more".

When the post is ready, **Send for approval** emails it to your reviewer. **Schedule** publishes it by itself at a time you choose, once it is switched on for your workspace. See [Scheduling](/help/scheduling).

## Improving a post

- **Edit it yourself.** Click **Edit** on the caption or the hashtags, change the text and click **Save**. Nothing is regenerated and it costs nothing. A counter shows how much room the platform leaves you. **Restore original** brings back the text AVIR AI wrote.
- **Try a different first line.** Click **Other openings** on the caption. AVIR AI suggests a few other first lines; click one to put it in place of the current first line, then **Save**. The rest of the caption stays as it is, and nothing changes until you save.
- **Just type the change.** *"Make it shorter"*, *"more formal"*, *"change the background to blue"*. The next message edits the post above.
- **Rewrite caption** writes the text again as a new version and keeps your image. Use the arrows (1 / 2) to switch between versions.
- **New image**, on the picture, makes a different picture and keeps the caption.
- **Refine this version** tells AVIR AI which version your next message should edit, useful after you've gone back to an earlier one.

> Tip: **Rewrite caption** only costs a new caption. **New image** uses one of today's images.

### The same post in other languages

Click **Translate** above the post, tick up to 5 languages and click **Create**. The list is English plus the languages of the Country on your [brand profile](/brand-profile).

- Each language is written the way a native speaker would post it, not word for word. Brand and product names, numbers, prices and links stay as they are, and about half the hashtags stay in English.
- Each one gets its own compliance check. The image stays the same.
- A tab per language appears above the post. Pick one to see, edit, schedule or send that language for approval on its own.
- Translations are versions of the same post: they use AI credit but don't count as new posts on your plan.

### Length limits

The caption and its hashtags are posted together and counted together.

| Platform | Characters | Hashtags |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 2,200 | 30 |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 | 30 |
| Facebook | 3,000 | 30 |

> Note: compliance notes describe the caption as it was checked. After you edit it by hand, the caption is checked again when you send it for approval.

### If something fails

When a post, a change or an image can't be created because the AI service was busy or the connection dropped, the message has a **Try again** button that repeats the same request.

## Conversations

The left panel lists your conversations. Each one remembers its posts and images, so you can come back and continue. **New Conversation** starts with a clean slate.
