Before a post goes out, send it to a manager or client. They see every platform's version in one email and decide on each.

## Sending a post for approval

1. In Studio Chat, click **Send for approval** on a result.
2. The first time, enter your **reviewer's email**. It is remembered; use **change** to send to someone else.
3. Check each platform's section. Untick a platform to leave it out.
4. For each platform choose **Text only**, **One image** or **Carousel**. For a carousel, click **Add slides** and pick images from the conversation in the order they should appear. Use the arrows to reorder.
5. Edit the caption and hashtags if needed, then click **Send for approval**.

The button then becomes **Sent for approval · View**, which opens the request.

## Platform rules

AVIR AI checks these before sending, so the reviewer only sees posts that can actually be published.

| Platform | Rule |
|---|---|
| Instagram | Needs an image. Carousels: 2 to 10 slides, all the same shape. At most 30 hashtags. |
| LinkedIn | Carousels: 2 to 20 slides. |
| Facebook | Carousels: 2 to 10 images. |

### LinkedIn carousel types

- **Multi-image post**: a post with several images that opens into a swipeable view.
- **Document carousel (PDF)**: the slides become a PDF people swipe through. The PDF is attached to the reviewer's email.

## Reviewing

The reviewer opens the link in the email and signs in. On the request they can:

- switch between platforms with the tabs, and swipe through carousel slides;
- click **Approve all**, or choose **Approve** or **Request changes** for each platform. A comment is required when asking for changes.

### Reminders

If your administrator has switched reminders on, a reviewer who hasn't decided gets a reminder email after 24 hours and a last one after 3 days. The request shows when a reminder was sent.

## After the decision

You get an email with the result for each platform. The request shows one of:

- **Approved**: every platform is approved.
- **Partly approved**: some platforms approved, changes asked on others.
- **Changes requested**: changes asked on every platform.

If changes were asked, edit the post in Studio Chat (click **Edit**, or type the change) and send it for approval again.

An approved platform can be published from the request with **Publish to** your account. See [Social accounts and publishing](/help/social-accounts).

Find all requests, past and pending, in [Approval requests](/approve).

> Tip: once Instagram is approved, a **Publish to Instagram** button appears on the request. See [Social accounts and publishing](/help/social-accounts).
