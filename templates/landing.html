FAQ

Questions about AVIR AI

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach the team at stradit.com.

What is AVIR AI?

AVIR AI is an AI social media studio from StradIT. You describe what you want to post, and it writes the caption, picks the hashtags and creates the image for LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook - all in your brand's voice.

How does AVIR AI learn my brand?

When you sign up you add your website. AVIR AI reads it to build your brand profile - products, tone of voice, colours and logo - and every post follows it. You can review and edit the profile at any time.

Which social media platforms are supported?

LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook. One image can be shared across all three, and posts can be scheduled to your connected accounts. Video generation is coming soon.

Will AVIR AI change or redraw my logo?

No. The AI never draws your logo - your actual logo file is placed on generated images, so it always looks exactly right.

Can my team review posts before they are published?

Yes. You can send a post for approval, and the reviewer gets an email to approve it or ask for changes before anything goes live.

Do I need design or copywriting skills?

No. Write a short brief in plain language, then refine the result in chat - for example "make it shorter" or "make the background blue" - until it is ready to post.
