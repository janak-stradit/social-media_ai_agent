## Remove one connected account (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube)

1. Sign in and go to Settings -> Social accounts.
2. Choose **Disconnect** next to the account. We delete its access token and stop publishing to it straight away.

You can also remove our access on the platform itself:

- Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings -> Security and login -> Business integrations (or Apps and websites) -> remove {product}.
- LinkedIn: Settings -> Data privacy -> Permitted services -> remove {product}.
- YouTube and Google: [Google security settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) -> {product} -> Remove access.

When access is removed on the platform, we can no longer use the token, and we delete it the next time we notice it no longer works.

## Delete your account and all its data

Email {email} from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". If you can't use that address, tell us your account email and we will check it is you.

We will:

- confirm your request within 3 working days;
- delete your account, brand profile, content, media library, contacts and connected-account tokens within 30 days, and from backups within a further 90 days;
- cancel any automatic renewal so you are not charged again.

We keep only what the law requires - invoices and payment records for tax (in India, at least 8 years) - and a note that the account was deleted.

Posts already published on social platforms stay there until you delete them on the platform; we cannot remove them for you once the account is disconnected.
