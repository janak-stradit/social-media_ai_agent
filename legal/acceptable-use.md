## You must not use the Service to

- break any law, or help someone else break it - including advertising, consumer protection, financial promotion, health claims and election rules in the countries you publish to;
- publish content that is false or misleading, including fake reviews, fake endorsements or claims you cannot support;
- infringe anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy or publicity rights - for example using another company's logo, or a person's photo or likeness, without permission;
- make deepfakes or content that pretends to be a real person or organisation, or that could be mistaken for official communication;
- create sexual content involving minors (never allowed, and reported to the authorities), sexually explicit content, or content that promotes violence, terrorism, self-harm or hatred against people for who they are;
- harass, threaten or bully anyone;
- send spam: emails or messages to people who have not agreed to receive them, or ignoring unsubscribe requests;
- break the rules of the platforms you publish to (Meta, LinkedIn, YouTube and others), including their rules on automation, engagement and paid partnerships;
- publish political or issue ads without the authorisations and labels the platform and the law require;
- attack or overload the Service, get around its limits or security, scrape it, or access other customers' data;
- resell or give access to the Service to others without our written agreement.

## AI content

- Review AI-generated text, images and videos before publishing. You are responsible for them as if you wrote or made them.
- Label AI content wherever a platform or law requires it.
- Don't ask the Service to copy a specific artist's or brand's work, or to put real people in situations they did not agree to.

## Regulated industries

If you work in a regulated field (for example finance, insurance, health, pharmaceuticals, legal, recruiting or alcohol), use the compliance settings on your brand profile and keep the required disclaimers in your posts. The Service's checks help but do not replace your own compliance review.

## What we do

We may remove content, stop a post from being published, limit features or suspend an account that breaks this policy, and report illegal content to the authorities. Where it is reasonable we will tell you first and let you fix it.
