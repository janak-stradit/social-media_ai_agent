## Delivery

{product} is an online service; nothing is shipped. A plan or top-up pack is added to your account as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a minute, and you get a receipt and invoice by email. If it has not appeared within an hour, write to {email} with your invoice number.

## Cancelling automatic renewal

- Go to Settings -> Plan & usage and choose **Cancel automatic renewal**, or write to {email}.
- Renewal stops straight away. You keep your plan and its allowance until the end of the month or year you have already paid for; after that the account moves to the Free plan. Your content is kept.
- To avoid the next charge, cancel at least one day before the renewal date shown in Settings. We email a reminder 3 days before each renewal.

## Refunds

| What you bought | Refund |
|---|---|
| First payment for a plan (monthly or yearly) | Full refund if you ask within 7 days of paying |
| Renewal of a plan | Full refund if you ask within 48 hours of the charge and have not used the renewed allowance |
| Top-up pack (images or video) | Full refund within 7 days if none of the pack has been used |
| Anything else, or a part-used period | No refund, except where the law gives you one |

If the Service was unavailable or did not work as described for a significant time, write to us and we will make a fair refund or credit even outside these limits.

## How refunds are paid

- Ask at {email} from your account's email address, with the invoice number.
- We answer within 3 working days. An approved refund goes back to the card or account you paid with through Stripe; it usually arrives in 5-10 working days, depending on your bank.
- A full refund removes what it paid for (the plan time or the unused pack). Indian customers get a GST credit note for every refund.

## Charge disputes

Please write to us before disputing a charge with your bank - it is faster. If a payment is disputed, the plan or pack it paid for may be paused until the dispute is settled.
