{company}, {address}, is responsible for the personal data described here. For the contacts and content you put into the Service (for example people on a contact list or in a photo), you decide how it is used and we process it for you, on your instructions.

## 2. What we collect

| What | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Account details | name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), account type, company | you |
| Brand profile | website address and what we read from your public website: company name, logo, colours, fonts, products, markets, contact pages | your website |
| Content | briefs, captions, images, videos, uploads, schedules, approvals and comments | you and the Service |
| Connected accounts | page, channel or profile name and id, access tokens, post results such as likes and reach | Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube - only after you connect them |
| Billing | plan, payments, invoices, GST number and billing address | you and Stripe (we never receive full card numbers) |
| Usage and security | sign-ins, IP address, browser type, actions in the account, errors | your device |
| Emails | whether you opened a link in our emails, your email preferences and unsubscribes | your device |

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18, and we do not ask for sensitive data (such as health or religion). Please don't put it into posts or briefs unless you need to.

## 3. Why we use it

- To provide the Service: create your brand profile, write and make posts, publish them to accounts you connected, and show results (contract).
- To take payments, issue GST invoices and keep tax records (contract and legal obligation).
- To keep the Service secure: prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised sign-ins (legitimate interest).
- To send service emails (receipts, renewal reminders, approval requests) and, if you have not switched them off, idea and product emails (legitimate interest or consent). Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
- To improve the Service from usage statistics (legitimate interest).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## 4. Artificial intelligence

To write and make content, we send your brief and the relevant parts of your brand profile to AI providers (section 5). We send only what is needed for the result and do not allow it to be used for advertising. Each provider's own terms on data use apply; they are linked from the [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) page. We keep a memory of your past campaigns inside your own account so new posts match your brand; it is not shared with other customers.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with providers who help run the Service, under contracts that protect it. The current list is on the [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) page. In short:

- hosting, storage and databases (Amazon Web Services);
- AI providers that write text and make images and videos;
- Stripe for payments;
- our email delivery provider;
- the social platforms you choose to publish to, which receive the posts you publish.

We may also share data if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of our business (you would be told).

## 6. Google and YouTube data

When you connect YouTube, we use Google's YouTube API Services to upload videos you choose and to read your channel's name and video results. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use that data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not let people read it except with your permission, for security, or as the law requires. You can remove our access at any time on the [Google security settings page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). See also the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 7. Meta and LinkedIn data

When you connect Facebook, Instagram or LinkedIn, we receive only the permissions you approve: to publish to the pages or profiles you choose and to read their post results. We use this data only to run those features for you. To remove it, disconnect the account in Settings or follow the steps on the [Data Deletion](/data-deletion) page.

## 8. How long we keep it

- Account, brand profile and content: while your account is open. After you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, and from backups within a further 90 days.
- Connected-account tokens: until you disconnect the account or delete your account.
- Invoices and payment records: for as long as tax law requires (in India, at least 8 years).
- Security logs: up to 12 months.

## 9. Where it is stored

Your data is currently stored on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States and may be processed in other countries where our providers work. When data leaves your country we use the safeguards the law requires, such as contractual protections.

## 10. Security

Passwords are hashed, two-step sign-in secrets are encrypted, traffic uses HTTPS, payments are handled by Stripe, and staff access is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal data, to object to or limit some uses, and to withdraw consent. Write to {email}; we answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for using your rights.

- **India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):** you can also nominate someone to act for you, and you can complain to {grievance_name} at {grievance_email}, who will respond within the time the law sets. If you are not satisfied, you can go to the Data Protection Board of India.
- **United Arab Emirates and GCC:** you have the rights given by the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and the laws of your country.
- **United States (for example California, Colorado, Virginia):** you have the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or targeted advertising (we do neither). An authorised agent may make a request for you.
- **EU and UK:** you can also complain to your data protection authority.

## 12. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to sign you in and keep the Service secure, and your browser's storage for preferences. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## 13. Changes

We will post any change here and update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will tell you by email or in the Service.
